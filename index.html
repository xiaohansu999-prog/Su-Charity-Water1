Our Story

In a small village in Africa, a young boy named Amari used to walk over 4 miles every day just to collect water from a dirty stream. The long journey made him late for school, and the water often made his family sick. One day, Water Charity partnered with the community to build a clean water well right in the center of the village. With clean water close to home, Amari no longer had to make the dangerous walk. Now, he goes to school on time, stays healthy, and plays with his friends instead of carrying heavy water buckets. His village is growing stronger and healthier — all because of the power of clean water.
